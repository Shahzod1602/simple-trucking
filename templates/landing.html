Our mission

Give every dispatcher
an AI co-pilot

Trucking dispatchers spend hours every day on repetitive work — typing rate confirmations, chasing drivers for status updates, copying data between systems. It's the part of the job nobody enjoys, and it's where most mistakes happen.

SimpleTrucking is building AI tools that handle the boring 80% — so dispatchers can focus on the 20% that actually grows the business: finding the right loads, building broker relationships, and supporting drivers.

Our goal: give every dispatcher in America — and eventually the world — an AI co-pilot that never sleeps, never mistypes a number, and never misses a check call.
